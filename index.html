About the Dose Rate Calculator

Gamma Knife dose rate drifts from estimated values due to source geometry effects beyond simple Co-60 decay. The community-fitted scale factor S corrects for this systematic difference.

DRcorrected = S × DRestimated
S = 0.9452 (fitted from 16 measurements across 2 institutions)
T½ = 1925.2 days (NIST Co-60, not Elekta's 5.26 yr)

Validated across Perfexion, Icon, and Esprit — same source assembly, same S. Five verified cases show ≤1% agreement with independent TLD measurements.

Machine physics data is cloud-safe. Patient data stays local — always.
